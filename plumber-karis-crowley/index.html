New-Home Plumbing Calls That Come Up

No hot water or lukewarm water

The water heater's thermostat or a tripped breaker is a common first suspect. If the tank is cold, water heater repair usually starts with the element or gas valve.

A drip under a sink

Supply lines and compression fittings can loosen over time. A small leak is cheaper to fix when caught on the cabinet floor than after the wood swells. Faucet repair often covers it.

Hard water spots and scale

Crowley's 2025 water report lists hardness of 6 to 9 grains per gallon. If your home is on that system, scale builds on fixtures, and a water softener is worth pricing.

Adding something the builder did not include

A new pot filler, a utility sink or a gas line for an outdoor kitchen is a plumbing installation job. Crowley has adopted the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), so ask what the permit office requires first.

A 15-Minute Plumbing Walkthrough for a New Home

Do this once. It catches most small problems before they become wet floors.

  • Locate the main shutoffTest that it turns, so you are not learning how during a leak.
  • Look under every sinkDry cabinet floors mean the supply lines are holding.
  • Check toilet basesA rocking toilet or a damp spot at the floor needs attention.
