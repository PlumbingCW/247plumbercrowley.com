Prices and Estimates Are Agreed by Phone

A clear price is explained and agreed with you by phone before work starts. Prices depend on factors such as the problem, the parts, access to the pipe, the time of day and the size of the job, and each service page lists the factors that apply. The website itself does not publish prices.

If the plumber finds something different on site, the new scope and price are discussed with you before any extra work begins. You are free to decline the work. Any fee that applies to a visit should be stated on the phone before the plumber is sent.

What Our Service Does Not Cover

We focus on plumbing. These jobs fall outside what we do, so please call the right specialist.

  • Septic tank pumping and septic system service.
  • Gas utility work, including meter and main line repairs, which belong to your gas utility.
  • Electrical work such as wiring, breakers and panels, which needs a licensed electrician.
  • Emergencies that call for 911 or your utility first, such as a gas smell or a fire.

Our services page lists the plumbing jobs we do handle.

Website Content and Your Responsibilities

The information on this site is general plumbing information for homeowners, not advice for your specific property.
