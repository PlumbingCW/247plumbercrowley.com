Old Town Burleson: A Depot Town With Older Plumbing Questions

Burleson grew up around a Katy railroad depot, and the first lot was sold in 1880. A fire in 1895 burned most of the business district, so the oldest part of Old Town was rebuilt after that. If you own or rent near Old Town, the building may have been remodeled more than once, and each remodel tends to add a different pipe material.

That layering is the practical point. A kitchen fed by 1/2 inch PEX can still tie into a galvanized branch behind the wall, and old steel narrows from the inside until one faucet loses pressure. Homes built before the mid-1990s may still have galvanized supply lines, polybutylene or cast iron drains, so we check what is in the wall before you approve a repair that assumes one material.

The Interurban Years and the I-35 Boom

The Interurban rail line ran through Burleson from 1912 to 1931, and I-35 opened through town in 1960. Each brought a wave of building. We will not guess the age of any one street, but a town that grew in waves has houses on very different plumbing standards side by side.

So two neighbors can call about the same leaking tank and need different work. One has a clean copper stub and a modern T&P valve, while the other has a corroded shutoff that will not close. We bring parts for either case and give you the price before the wrench turns.

Before We Arrive in Burleson

Emergency calls here usually involve a tank or a clogged line. These four moves limit the damage.

Close the cold supply.

Find the shutoff on the cold line above the tank and turn it clockwise until it stops. If it spins loose or will not move, do not force it, and tell us when you call.

Cut power or gas to the heater.

Flip the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas control to off. A heater running dry burns out its elements. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

Stop sending water down the drain.

When a line is backing up, no flushing, no dishwasher, no laundry. Every gallon you add will surface at the lowest fixture in the house.

Clear a path and call.

Move boxes away from the heater or cleanout. Then call our 24/7 line at (817) 601-6266 and give us your ZIP, 76028 or 76058.

Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Burleson

Hardness of 5 to 10 grains per gallon leaves a chalky layer on the bottom of a tank, which is why elements burn out and tanks rumble. When a unit is past 8 to 12 years old, the repair-versus-replace question comes up. A plumber checks the anode rod, T&P valve and tank wall, then tell you which side of that line you are on.

Drain Cleaning Along Old Lines and New Lots

Kitchen grease and shower hair cause most slow drains. A snake clears a soft clog in minutes, but roots and heavy buildup call for cable cutters or hydro jetting. If the same drain clogs twice in a season, the pipe may be bellied or cracked, and a camera will show it.

Start with drain cleaning for one fixture. When several fixtures back up together, the problem is the main line, not a trap, and that is the moment to stop running water and call.
