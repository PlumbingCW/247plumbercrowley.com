Emergency Faucet Repair in Crowley, TX

Faucet repair in Crowley, TX becomes an emergency when a valve will not shut off or water is leaking into a wall or cabinet. Our line is open 24/7. Close the small oval-handled stop valve under the sink, or the main water valve if there is no stop, and call us.

Most faucet problems are small parts: a worn cartridge, a cracked washer, a set of O-rings or a mineral-clogged aerator. A drip that wastes a gallon an hour is no emergency, but a shower valve that cannot be turned off is. Here is how we sort out which one you have.

Valve Will Not Shut Off? Stop the Water First

If water keeps running when the handle is off, or it sprays from behind a wall, work through this list in order.

  • Close the stop valveTurn the oval handle under the sink clockwise until it stops. Do the same on the hot and cold stops for a toilet, washer or laundry sink.
  • Use the main valve if there is no stopShower and tub valves often have no local stop. Close the main valve where the line enters the house, then open a low faucet to drain the pressure.
  • Protect the floorLay towels along the base of the cabinet or wall, move rugs and, if the water reaches an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker. Do not touch a wet outlet.
  • Do not force a seized handleA handle that spins freely or will not move can strip the stem and turn a cartridge swap into a valve replacement. Stop and call.
  • Take a photo, then callA picture of the faucet, its brand mark and the water damage helps us bring the right part. We can often schedule a same-day visit, and our dispatch is open after hours.

How Crowley Water Wears Out Faucets

The City of Crowley's 2025 Water Quality Report gives total hardness of 108 to 150 mg/L, which is 6 to 9 grains per gallon, and pH of 7.8 to 8.5. Hard water leaves white scale on aerators and inside cartridges. The scale narrows the outlet, so water sprays sideways or pressure falls. Slightly alkaline water also scales faster once it is heated, so hot-side cartridges tend to clog first.

Those same minerals scratch the soft rubber seals on a cartridge, so a single-handle faucet may start to drip long before the chrome looks worn. If your aerator is crusty, unscrew it, soak it in vinegar for an hour and brush it clean. That fixes weak flow, but it does not fix a leak at the base of the spout or under the handle.

Fixtures and Valves We Repair

The fix depends on the fixture. Here is what we commonly see around Crowley homes.

Kitchen and bathroom faucets

Drips from the spout usually mean a worn cartridge, ceramic disc or washer. We replace the part when it is available, or the whole faucet when the body is corroded.

Shower valves

A shower that drips after you turn it off, or loses hot water, usually needs a new cartridge or a mixing valve. Access is through the wall or from behind the tub.

Bathtub faucets and diverters

A tub spout that leaks when the shower runs is a failed diverter. Leaks at the handle point to the cartridge, and leaks inside the wall need a closer look.

Outdoor spigots

Frost-proof hose bibs and standard spigots leak at the stem or the vacuum breaker. Water running inside the wall behind the spigot means the pipe is damaged, so shut off the line.

Supply stops and shutoff valves

Old multi-turn valves seize or drip at the stem. We replace them with quarter-turn valves so you can shut off water fast next time.

Laundry and utility sinks

Washing machine valves and utility faucets leak at the stem or the hose. We can swap the valve and braided lines in one visit.

Leaks That Look Like Faucet Problems

Sometimes the faucet is fine and the pipe behind it is not. A wet wall below a tub spout, a stain on the ceiling under an upstairs bath, or a meter that keeps moving points to a leak inside the wall. In that case, see our page on leak detection or, if the pipe has failed, burst pipe repair.
