Emergency Calls Versus Planned Work

An emergency is anything that is damaging your home right now or making it unsafe. That includes a burst pipe, a sewer backup, a tank leaking onto the floor, and a main line that will not drain. For those, close the main valve, stop using water, and call (866) 310-6010, because our line is open 24/7.

Planned work is anything you can schedule: a tank you want to replace before it fails, a water softener for Crowley's 6 to 9 grain water, or a new fixture install during a remodel. Planned jobs get cleaner results because we can order parts, check permit needs and finish in a single visit.

Whichever service you pick, the work is done to the plumbing code the city has adopted. Crowley uses the 2021 International Plumbing Code with local amendments, which is the standard an inspector would check against.

When a small problem becomes an emergency

A slow drain is a nuisance. A drain that backs up into a second fixture is a mainline problem. A dripping tank valve is a repair, but a tank that weeps from the seam is done and will fail soon.

When it can wait for morning

A dripping faucet, a running toilet you can shut off at the stop, or a noisy pipe can usually wait. Close the local stop valve, put a bucket under it, and book a faucet repair for the next day.

Why Crowley Water Shapes Which Service You Need

The City of Crowley's 2025 water quality report lists total hardness of 108 to 150 mg/L, which is 6 to 9 grains per gallon, with pH between 7.8 and 8.5. Scale builds on heating elements and in tank bottoms, which shortens tank life and slows hot water. A tankless water heater needs periodic descaling in this water, and a softener helps both types.

Chloramine is the disinfectant, and Crowley buys its treated water from Fort Worth. Chloramine is tougher on some rubber parts than plain chlorine, so flappers and supply hoses are worth checking when they age. Older homes with polybutylene or galvanized lines may need whole house repiping, and the code that applies to new work is the 2021 International Plumbing Code as adopted by Crowley.
