What to Have Ready When You Call

A minute of preparation helps us send the right plumber with the right parts. You do not need technical words, just the facts as you see them.

  • Your street address and ZIP. Crowley is 76036, and we also serve areas like 76028, 76140 and 76119, so the ZIP helps us place you.
  • What you see, hear or smell. A dripping ceiling, a gurgling toilet, a hissing water heater or a wet spot in the yard each point to a different cause.
  • Where the main water shutoff is. It is often at the meter box near the street or on an exterior wall of the house. If you can turn it off safely, say so.
  • Whether anything is still running. Tell us if water is still spreading, if a drain is backing up, or if the water heater is warm or cold.
  • The age and type of the appliance. For a water heater, a tank label showing gas or electric and a 40 or 50 gallon size saves time on the phone.

What Happens After You Call

We listen to the problem, take your address, and match you with plumbers who cover your part of the area. Before anyone starts work, the price approach is explained and agreed with you by phone. We offer same-day appointments when available, and we do not promise a specific arrival time.

While you wait, we will tell you what to do. For a burst pipe that usually means shutting the main valve and opening a low faucet to drain the lines. For a sewer backup it means stopping all water use and keeping kids and pets away from the spill, with no bleach or chemical drain openers poured into the backup.

If the problem turns out to be outside our scope, we tell you straight away. We do not pump septic tanks, do gas utility work or do electrical work, and our about page explains why.

Gas Smell? Do Not Call Us First

If you smell gas, leave the building right away and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not flip switches or use a phone indoors. Once the utility says it is safe, call a plumber about the appliance or pipe that needs repair.

Who to Call for Utility and City Issues

Some problems belong to the city, not a plumber. For an after-hours water main break or a wastewater backup in the street, the City of Crowley directs callers to Police Dispatch at 817-297-2276. Public Works is at 817-297-2201 during the day. Ask Public Works where the city side of your water line ends, since that decides who pays for a repair.

For permits and inspections, Crowley Community Development is at 201 E. Main Street, with the permits office on extension 3000 of that same number. Office hours are Monday to Thursday from 7:30 in the morning to 5:30 in the evening. On Fridays the office closes at 11:30 in the morning.
