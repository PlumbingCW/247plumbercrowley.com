Business Flooding or Backup After Hours: First Moves

A backing-up floor drain at 9 p.m. needs calm, quick steps.

  1. Find and close the main valve.

    Know where the water main enters the building before you need it. Close it fully and open one faucet to relieve pressure.

  2. Stop the load on the drains.

    Turn off dishwashers, ice machines and handwashing stations that feed a backing-up drain. Post a sign on restrooms.

  3. Clear the floor and cut the power.

    Move stock and equipment off the floor. If water is near outlets, panels or refrigeration, switch off those circuits at the breaker and do not touch wet electrical gear.

  4. Take photos and note the time.

    Photos of standing water, damaged goods and the drain support an insurance claim. Write down what failed and when.

  5. Call the 24/7 line.

    Call (866) 310-6010 and tell us the type of business, which drain is backing up and whether the water is clean or dirty. We schedule same-day or after-hours service when available.

Commercial Jobs We Take

Callers in Crowley ask about a short list of things again and again.

Commercial drains

Restroom, kitchen and mop sink drains clog from paper, grease and debris. We cable and camera the line, and for heavy buildup use hydro-jetting.

Floor drains

Floor drains overflow when the trap is blocked or the branch is backed up. We clear the drain, check trap seals and look at slope and cleanout access.

Grease traps and interceptors

Kitchens need a trap that is pumped and cleaned before it fills. Local grease rules vary, so ask Crowley Public Works what applies to your business.

Backflow testing and repair

Backflow preventers on irrigation, fire lines and kitchens protect the public water supply. We test, repair and replace assemblies.

Lift pumps and ejectors

A sewage ejector pit with a failed float or pump floods a restroom below grade. We replace pumps, floats and check valves.

Restrooms and fixtures

Flush valves, urinals, lavatories and water heaters all fail at the worst time. See toilet repair and water heater repair.

Irrigation Backflow Rules Set by Crowley

Crowley's local amendment to Section 608.17.5 of the 2021 International Plumbing Code says no valve may be installed downstream of an atmospheric vacuum breaker on a lawn irrigation system. It also says chemical injection requires a reduced pressure principal backflow preventer.

That matters for a business with landscaping and a sprinkler system: an injector for fertilizer on the line changes the type of preventer you need. Permits go through Crowley Community Development at 201 E. Main Street, and permit rules vary, so ask the building department before you change or add equipment.
