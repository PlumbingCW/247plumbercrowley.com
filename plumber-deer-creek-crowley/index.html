Whose Pipe Is It: Meter, Street and House

A typical residential setup has a city main under the street, a service line to a meter, and your own line from the meter to the house. Which side owns which section varies, and the City of Crowley pages do not say where the city's responsibility ends. Ask Public Works at 817-297-2201 before you pay for a repair near the meter.

If your bill comes from Fort Worth instead of Crowley, the same question goes to Fort Worth Water. The City of Crowley itself buys its treated water from Fort Worth, so the water may be the same, while the billing contact differs. Work after the meter falls under the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that Crowley has adopted.

Water Line Problems That Show Up Near the Meter

A meter that moves with taps off

This is the clearest sign of a leak after the meter. Shut the main valve, and if the dial stops, the leak is on your side. Leak detection finds where.

A soft, wet patch in the lawn

Water breaking through soil often leaves a lush, soggy strip that stays green in dry weather. A water line repair may mean a short trench.

Pressure that is too high or too low

A failing pressure regulator can cause banging and early fixture wear. Low pressure at every tap may mean a partly closed valve or a leak.

Scale on faucets and kettles

The City of Crowley's 2025 report lists hardness of 6 to 9 grains per gallon. A softener or filter reduces the scale if your home is on that supply.

What to Do When You Find a Wet Spot

  1. Shut off the water.

    Close the main valve at the house. If the wet spot stops growing, the leak is on your side of the system.

  2. Take a photo and note the meter.

    Photograph the wet area and write down the meter reading. Both help a plumber and your utility.

  3. Call the right party.

    A leak on the street side of the meter goes to the utility. A leak after the meter is a plumbing job.

  4. Check the water heater too.

    A water heater leak can look like a service line problem. Look at the base of the tank before you dig.
